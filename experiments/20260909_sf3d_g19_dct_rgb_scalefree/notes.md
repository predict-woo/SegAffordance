# 20260909_sf3d_g19_dct_rgb_scalefree — SF3D 3D-DCT from scratch, RGB-only, scale-free head

**Question:** the depth ablation at equal recipe: `20260821_sf3d_g19_dct` (scratch, depth input, MA 25.98 / PDet 21.72 / mIoU 0.2685) vs the same recipe with NO depth (use_depth false, load_depth false) and the scale-free head (metres = GT z0 · Δ̃ at train, z_p · Δ̃ at test). Pairs with `20260909_sf3d_g19_dct_rgb_scalefree_ft_hoi4d` (same model, HOI4D init) to separate "depth" from "HOI4D transfer". Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-09-rgb-only-scale-free-trajectory-design.md.

**Recipe:** `config/sf3d_train_runpod_g19_dct_rgb_scalefree.yaml` (30 ep, lr 1e-5, DCT-6 head, scratch; `trajectory_scale_source gt_z0`, `test_trajectory_scale pred_z_p`). Pod B (RTX PRO 6000 Server), 3 h 35 min. Tested twice: pred_z_p (logs/test.log) and gt_z0 oracle scale (logs/test_gt_z0.log).

**Result:** best val/loss_total **1.0791** (ep 20; depth scratch 0.9652). Test (5,088): MA **24.88** / signed 24.71 (depth 25.98 / 25.83), PDet **17.39** (21.72), mIoU **0.2425** (0.2685), point err 0.1068, point3d 0.269 m (0.259), origin 0.324 m (0.276), axis all 29.6° / matched 22.0° (25.3 / 18.2), rot flips 14.7, traj_dir 92.0, roughness 0.0109 (0.0090). Val curves: L_trajectory (normalized, on z0·Δ̃ — the same quantity as the depth run's) best 0.468 vs 0.348, L_mask 0.333 vs 0.325, L_point_3d 0.040 vs 0.035. **The two test passes are identical on every reported metric** (MA is the type+axis pass rate on IoU-matched samples; direction / roughness / proj2d are scale-invariant; point3d reads z_p) — the test suite has no metric-trajectory-error number, so the predicted-vs-oracle scale question is only answered by val L_trajectory, which uses GT z0.

**Reading:** on SF3D, depth is worth about 1.1 MA, 4.3 PDet, 0.026 mIoU, 4° of axis, 5 cm of origin and a third of the trajectory val loss (0.35 → 0.47). Masks lose the least (val mask loss nearly equal — the mIoU/PDet gap is mostly the point/detection side), the 3D geometry heads (trajectory, origin, axis) lose the most — as expected: those are the quantities the depth map was directly informative for, and the RGB-only model has to infer them from appearance. This is the honest price of the RGB-only design on the one dataset that had good depth; the HOI4D arm showed the price is zero on the 2D datasets.

**Decision:** keep the RGB-only scale-free model as THE model for the multi-dataset line (user decision: no depth anywhere); this row is its scratch baseline on SF3D (replaces 25.98 as the number to beat for RGB-only inits). Single seed. Ckpt best-epoch20-valloss1.0791.ckpt on the volume.
