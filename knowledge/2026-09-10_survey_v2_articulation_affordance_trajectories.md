# Survey v2 — How the articulated-object / affordance / VLM-manipulation literature predicts manipulation trajectories (and what heads it uses)

*Fresh web survey, 2026-09-10. Scope: methods that turn one image / point cloud (+ instruction) into a motion of a movable part or an interaction trajectory. Written for the SegAffordance trajectory head (20×3 camera-frame, scale-free deltas; "plain" MLP vs 6-coef DCT readout).*

---

## 0. TL;DR

1. **Almost nobody in the articulated-object literature regresses a free-form 3D trajectory from a pooled feature.** The dominant pattern is *predict articulation parameters (type, axis, origin) → render the trajectory analytically* (Rodrigues rotation about the axis / translation along it). FlowBot++, GAMMA, RPMArt, UniAff/A3VLM, "Opening Articulated Objects in the Real World", Kinematic-aware prompting and the factor-graph line of work all do this. The one classic free-form regressor (VAT-Mart) uses a cVAE over 5 six-DoF waypoints with a residual (delta) parameterisation and a separate scorer.
2. The strongest OOD evidence for the analytic route is FlowBot++: the *same* PointNet++ backbone, flow-only readout gives normalised distance 0.73 on held-out categories, axis-projection-only 0.27, combined analytic 0.18. GAMMA beats VAT-Mart on unseen categories by 2×–5× on doors/drawers with an analytic screw trajectory.
3. Where a free-form curve is kept, recent work parameterises it *geometrically and anchored*: AFUN (2026) predicts K Bézier control points anchored at the contact centroid and reports Bézier > OPD-style parameterisation on SceneFun3D-derived 3D motion; A0 predicts 5 post-contact 2D waypoints with a DiT diffusion head; EgoMAN predicts 3 semantic waypoints then a flow-matching motion expert.
4. Deterministic regression of multimodal motion (push vs pull, left vs right hinge) produces mode-averaged squiggles; generative heads (FlowBotHD diffusion over flow; "What Happens Next?" rectified-flow over trajectory grids: −28 % MSE vs regressors) fix this. This is a plausible root cause of our OOD "squiggle while axis stays right".
5. VLM-style tokenised traces (HAMSTER, MolmoAct, Magma, Embodied-R1, 3D HAMSTER) work because a large VLM backbone supplies priors and RL (GRPO with RMSE reward) can be applied; they are 2D (or (u,v,d)) polylines of 1–8 points. They are the wrong tool for a small task-specific model and 2D-only traces distort depth (3D HAMSTER: +6 pts by adding depth).

## 2. The three families, in prose

### 2.1 Parameter heads + analytic rendering (the articulated-object mainstream)

**Point-cloud manipulation.** FlowBot3D (2205.04382) showed a dense per-point "articulation flow" (direction each point moves under an infinitesimal joint displacement, PointNet++ dense head, L2) generalises across categories better than policy learning, but the flow only gives an *instantaneous* direction and must be re-predicted every step. FlowBot++ (2306.12893) is the key paper for us: it adds a second dense output, the *articulation projection* r_p (vector from each point to the axis), recovers the axis as ω=(r×f)/‖r×f‖ and origin v=p+r, Gram-Schmidt-orthogonalises the two predictions, and then **renders K smooth waypoints with Rodrigues' formula** p'=R(φ)(p−v)+v without replanning. Their ablation is the cleanest evidence in the field that an analytic decoder fixes OOD trajectory collapse: on held-out categories flow-only 0.73, projection-only 0.27, combined 0.18 normalised distance, versus 0.02–0.08 on train categories. GAMMA (2309.16264) does the same with per-point projection vectors + axis directions voted through DBSCAN, then plans P=cosθ·p+(1−cosθ)uuᵀp+sinθ[u]×p+q (revolute) or p+δu (prismatic), and *re-fits* the parameters online by Hungarian-matching planned vs executed trajectories; it reports 8.85° axis error on unseen categories (ANCSH 26.8°) and 86.9 % door-push on unseen categories vs VAT-Mart 38.1 %. RPMArt (2403.16023) votes axis/origin/affordable point from point tuples and again computes the trajectory as "rotate δ about (u,q) / translate δ along u". The system paper "Opening Articulated Objects in the Real World" (2402.17767) uses Mask R-CNN for handle + axis, analytic quarter-circle arcs / straight lines, and finds *modular beats end-to-end trained on 1000+ demos* with perception (not the trajectory) as the bottleneck; the factor-graph line (2601.01438, 2409.16287, ArtiPoint 2509.01708) keeps the screw model and just updates it from interaction.

**Single-image / scene parameter heads** never output trajectories at all; they output (type, axis, origin). OPD (2203.16421) attaches FC layers to Mask R-CNN RoI features: CE for type, smooth-L1 for axis and origin with weights [1,8,8], origin loss only for revolute, and finds predicting in an object-canonical frame (OPDRCNN-O) is more consistent across viewpoints than camera frame. OPDMulti (2303.14087) moves to Mask2Former queries → MLPs, with per-part pose (-P); on real multi-object images motion mAP falls from 32.9 (detection) to 16.0 (+axis+origin), i.e. the axis/origin regression is the hard part. SceneFun3D's Mask3D-FM baseline (CVPR 2024) is the same recipe on 3D scenes (CE + smooth-L1 axis + smooth-L1 origin; AP25 26.6 → 7.9 with type+axis+origin at 15°/0.25 thresholds); USDNet (Articulate3D, 2412.01398) improves it with a cosine axis loss, an *origin-to-axis distance* loss (origin is only defined up to sliding along the axis) and averaging point-wise dense predictions over the instance mask, echoing Ditto's finding that dense per-point voting beats a single global regression. 3DOI (2305.09664) is the single-RGB analogue: a SAM-style query transformer predicting movable mask, rigid/articulated, rotation/translation, the axis as a *2D line in the image*, and an affordance heatmap, trained on internet/egocentric images. The VLM variants (A3VLM 2406.07549, UniAff 2409.20551) emit bbox + axis as two 3D points + type in text and then apply hand-written primitives; UniAff reports +9.6 % on unseen categories over A3VLM. Articulate-Anything (2410.13882) and Real2Code (2406.08474) push this to code generation (URDF joints), with 75 % joint-prediction success and 0.141 rad axis error.

**Takeaway.** Across all of these, the *trajectory* is a deterministic function of (type, axis, origin, extent). Nobody reports a free-form trajectory head outperforming this on unseen categories.

### 2.2 Learned trajectory heads (cVAE, diffusion, spline)

VAT-Mart (2106.14440) is the canonical learned trajectory proposal for articulated objects: per-point cVAE (128-d latent, MLP decoder 320→512→256→30) emitting 5 waypoints × 6-DoF *in residual form* (wp0, wp1−wp0, …), trained with L1 on positions + 6D-rotation loss + KL, plus a separate BCE trajectory scorer and an actionability head supervised by the top-5 scores; the target motion magnitude θ (10–70° or 0.1–0.7 of shape scale) is an *input*. Cross-category success drops 15–20 points, worse than GAMMA's analytic route on the same tasks. ActAIM2 (2410.20258) replaces the Gaussian latent by a Gaussian-mixture cVAE to get discrete modes and classifies discretised keyposes; AdaManip (2502.11124) uses a DDPM diffusion policy over sparse 6-D keyframes; FlowBotHD (2410.07078) diffuses the dense flow field with a small DiT (5 layers, 128 hidden) conditioned on history and improves fully-closed-door success from 23 % to 77 % — the point being that *ambiguity* (push vs pull, left vs right hinge) is what breaks deterministic regressors.

The most relevant recent design is AFUN (2606.02551, "affordance foundation model"): RGB-D + language → task-conditional mask + a **3D post-contact motion curve parameterised as K Bézier control points in relative coordinates, anchored at the masked contact centroid**, predicted by a 6-layer transformer decoder that cross-attends 3D features (Sonata), SAM3 object features and 32 learned "MetaQuery" tokens from Qwen3-VL; the loss is L1 on 16 uniformly sampled points of the curve (λ=100). They evaluate on 721 SceneFun3D elements (ADE_rel 0.135) and, importantly, ablate the curve parameterisation: Bézier (0.254 ADE) beats an OPD-style axis/type parameterisation (0.282), attributing the loss to "single parameterisation for multiple motion types … introduces ambiguity" — i.e. a badly-gated analytic decoder can lose to a smooth free curve. A0 (2504.12636) predicts contact + 5 post-contact 2D waypoints with a DiT diffusion head (MSE), pre-trained on 1 M contact points; EgoMAN (2512.16907) decouples *semantic waypoints* (start/contact/end as VLM tokens) from a flow-matching motion expert and shows the explicit waypoint interface beats an implicit embedding.

For scene-generic motion, "What Happens Next?" (ICLR 2026, 2509.21592) generates a dense grid of 2D point trajectories with a trajectory-VAE + rectified-flow DiT conditioned on DINOv2 tokens and beats the deterministic regressors ATM / Tra-MoE by ~28–30 % MSE on LIBERO, explicitly because regression averages over futures. MotionForesight (2607.16192) shows the deterministic alternative (adapter on a 3D tracker, MSE, reference-anchored 3D tracks for 15 future frames) still generalises OOD when trained on 40 K human videos.

### 2.3 VLM / LLM traces

RT-Trajectory (2311.01977) established that a coarse 2D trajectory sketch (red channel = time, green = height, circles = gripper events) is a better conditioning signal than language (67 % vs 16.7 % on 7 unseen skills) — but the sketch is an *input*. Prediction of traces came with LLARVA (2D coordinate tokens as an auxiliary target, +15 % avg on RLBench), Magma (Trace-of-Mark: CoTracker on a 15×15 grid, static points <2 px dropped, coordinates quantised to 256 bins; removing SoM/ToM halves SimplerEnv-Bridge 35.4→17.7), HAMSTER (VILA-13B emitting [(x,y,gripper)] paths simplified by RDP, trained on 1.2 M samples; the path is then drawn on the image for RVT-2/3D-DA; Colosseum 0.43 vs 0.18), MolmoAct (depth tokens → 1–5-point polyline in 0–255 bins → action tokens, all next-token CE; +23.3 OOD over π0-FAST; users can edit the trace) and Embodied-R1 (exactly 8 equidistant 2D points, GRPO with reward 0.9·(1−normalised RMSE)+0.1·format; RL 69.5 vs SFT 41.3 on OOD pointing). 3D HAMSTER (IROS 2026, 2606.31329) is the corrective: 2D pixel traces "inherit whatever depth lies beneath them", so it emits (u,v,d) tuples with a dedicated depth encoder + L1 depth-reconstruction auxiliary; 5 cm accuracy goes 0.7 %→42.6 % and success 38.8→44.8 over 2D HAMSTER. Two cautionary results: "VLMs Trace Without Tracking" (2605.15672) shows even frontier VLMs fail basic visual path-following through local competition, and TraceVLA finds concatenating raw history frames *hurts* while overlaid traces help. ManipLLM (2312.16217) is the articulated-object member of this family: text output of contact pixel + gripper up/forward directions (100 bins), then *no learned trajectory* — subsequent waypoints come from an active impedance probe that follows the direction of maximal movement. VoxPoser / CoPa / ReKep never learn a trajectory head at all: the LLM writes cost functions and a planner produces the motion.

## 3. What this means for our failure modes

- **Plain head noise (roughness 0.07).** No paper regresses 20 independent 3-vectors from a pooled feature. Every learned head imposes structure: residual/delta parameterisation (VAT-Mart), 5 waypoints (VAT-Mart, A0), Bézier control points anchored at the contact (AFUN), 3 semantic waypoints + smooth generator (EgoMAN), or an analytic arc (FlowBot++/GAMMA). Our DCT-6 is the cheapest member of this class, but it is *not anchored to geometry*, so smoothness does not imply correctness.
- **OOD squiggles with correct axis.** Two separate mechanisms in the literature: (a) the trajectory head has no access to the axis geometry, so it cannot inherit the axis head's generalisation (FlowBot3D flow-only 0.73 vs FlowBot++ 0.18 is exactly this gap); (b) multimodality (direction sign, push/pull) collapses to the mean under L2 (FlowBotHD, "What Happens Next?"). Both are fixable without touching the backbone.
- **2D-projection supervision.** HAMSTER→3D HAMSTER shows that 2D-only trajectory supervision leaves depth unconstrained; our unit-depth-anchored projection loss on HOI4D/EPIC/ARCTIC has the same blind spot, which an analytic decoder removes because depth evolution is then dictated by the axis.

## 4. Ranked design options for the SegAffordance trajectory readout

### #1 — Analytic screw decoder with predicted extent (+ small gated residual)  [recommended]
**Design.** Keep the type/axis/origin heads. Add scalar heads: extent θ (revolute, radians) or δ (prismatic, in scale-free units) and optionally a 20-step monotone progress profile s_k = cumsum(softmax(·)) (default linear). Render
- revolute: x_k = R(s_k θ; u)(x_0 − o) + o, with x_0 the interaction point (unit-depth anchor as today);
- prismatic: x_k = x_0 + s_k δ u;
- mix with the type probability (soft) during training, argmax at test; output scale-free deltas exactly as now. Optional residual: 3–4 Bézier control points (AFUN) or our DCT-6, added with a penalty λ‖residual‖ so it stays small; drop it OOD if unsure (FlowBot++ has none).

**Why it helps.** The trajectory *inherits* whatever generalisation the axis head has — the observed failure mode ("axis right, trajectory squiggles") becomes impossible by construction; roughness is zero; the SF3D axis-consistency loss becomes an identity; the 2D-track projection loss on hand-video data now back-propagates into axis/origin/extent (a new supervision path for axis from 2D videos, as in FlowBot++ where the same L2 trains both). Depth ambiguity of the 2D loss is removed because depth is dictated by the arc.
**Evidence.** FlowBot++ 0.73→0.18 OOD; GAMMA vs VAT-Mart on unseen categories (door-pull 63.9 vs 12.9 %); "Opening in the real world" modular > e2e; RPMArt/UniAff/A3VLM all deploy this way; AFUN's warning that a poorly gated single parameterisation can lose to Bézier (0.282 vs 0.254) argues for the soft type gate + residual.
**Cost.** ~50 lines, two scalar heads, no new data. Risk: origin error propagates into the arc radius; mitigate with USDNet's origin-to-axis-distance loss and 3DOI/OPD-style camera-frame origin (origin is only defined up to sliding along u).

### #2 — Multimodal generator over the low-dim parameters (extent sign/magnitude, optional residual)
**Design.** Replace the deterministic extent (+residual) regression by a small cVAE (VAT-Mart: z=128, min-of-N / KL) or a 5-layer DiT/flow-matching denoiser (FlowBotHD size: 128 hidden) over [θ or δ, residual coefs], conditioned on the 512-d fused feature; keep a scoring head (VAT-Mart BCE) or use mean-of-k (What-Happens-Next "MeanT, k=8") at inference.
**Why.** Addresses mode averaging (push/pull, open direction) that deterministic L2 produces — the likely source of squiggles that survive smoothing. Because the generative variable is ≤10-d after #1, this is cheap.
**Evidence.** What Happens Next −28 % MSE vs regressors; FlowBotHD 23→77 % on ambiguous doors; Where2Act/VAT-Mart min-of-N; EgoMAN flow-matching expert 0.273→0.162 ADE.
**Cost.** Moderate (sampling at inference, mode selection); only worthwhile after #1 or on top of a Bézier head.

### #3 — Anchored Bézier / spline control-point head (AFUN) as the free-form fallback
**Design.** K=4–6 control points in the unit-depth-anchored frame with P_0 fixed at the interaction point, L1 on 16–20 sampled points; a transformer decoder query attending to mask features rather than a pooled vector (AFUN, 6 layers; ours could be 2).
**Why.** Same smoothness as DCT-6 but anchored, monotone-friendly, and evaluated on our exact data (SceneFun3D ADE_rel 0.135 with n=721). Use as the residual in #1 or as the non-articulated ("freedom object", buttons, plugs) branch where an axis is meaningless.
**Evidence.** AFUN Bézier vs OPD-parameterisation ablation; A0's 5-waypoint diffusion; VAT-Mart residual waypoints.
**Cost.** Low; a like-for-like swap for DCT.

### #4 — Dense flow / projection auxiliary head over the mask
**Design.** Add a 6-channel per-pixel head on the mask decoder: articulation flow f (unit direction of motion of each pixel of the movable part at t=0) and projection r (vector to the axis), supervised analytically from SF3D (axis, origin, type) and, for 2D datasets, from CoTracker/GT tracks (Magma/TraceVLA-style, static pixels dropped). Recover axis by voting (ω=(r×f)/‖·‖, o=p+r averaged over the mask, USDNet/Ditto dense-average), feed into #1.
**Why.** Dense supervision on every mask pixel instead of one pooled vector; couples mask and motion; voting is far more robust than a single regression (Ditto, USDNet, GAMMA, FlowBot++).
**Evidence.** FlowBot++ combined 0.18 vs proj-only 0.27; Ditto dense > global; SceneFun3D Mask3D-FM's weak +MAO (7.9) is the global-regression failure mode this avoids.
**Cost.** Medium: new decoder channels, analytic GT generation; no architecture rewrite.

### #5 — Tokenised autoregressive trace (MolmoAct / HAMSTER / Embodied-R1 style)  [not recommended now]
**Design.** Quantise (u,v,d) into 256 bins (3D HAMSTER) and predict 8–20 tokens autoregressively; optional GRPO with an RMSE reward (Embodied-R1).
**Why it might help.** Classification over bins is robust to outliers and supports RL refinement; ManipLLM's 100-bin direction output is a small-scale version.
**Why not for us.** The gains in this family come from a 3–13 B VLM backbone and 10⁵–10⁶ trace samples; our model is small and single-image; 2D-only traces distort depth (3D HAMSTER); VLMs are demonstrably bad at path continuation (2605.15672); and none of these papers evaluates on articulated-part motion. A cheap borrow is to *classify* extent θ into bins (ManipLLM/ActAIM2) instead of regressing it, inside #1.

**Ordering rationale.** #1 removes the observed failure mode structurally at near-zero cost and turns the 2D-video loss into axis supervision; #3 provides the residual/fallback; #2 fixes mode-averaging once the output is low-dimensional; #4 strengthens the axis that everything now depends on; #5 is a different research programme.
