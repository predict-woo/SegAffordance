Reads: 10% of 3D data alone cannot buy a perception trunk (masks near zero in both versions) while articulation heads degrade gracefully — and the 2D pretrain supplies exactly the missing trunk (masks ×10, detection ×14–30). Verdict both times: 2D+10% ≫ 10% alone, still short of 100% — the remaining gap is concentrated in MA and origin, i.e. quantities only 3D labels teach.

Reads: the coupling is asymmetric — removing the trajectory (B) costs 7.8 MA and doubles rot flips, but removing articulation (C) costs the trajectory nothing. L_pp is a trade, not a free win: on this family it buys type accuracy and sign stability at the cost of ~2 MA (and the trade reverses on the fdiff family — no universal rule).
