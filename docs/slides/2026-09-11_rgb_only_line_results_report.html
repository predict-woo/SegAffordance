The RGB-only line: results report

Everything since the model went depth-free on 2026-09-09: the design, every run, the curves, the panels, and what to do next.

2026-09-09 to 2026-09-11 · single seed throughout · SF3D test = 5,088 samples; hand sources on their held-out scene splits · commits f6536b3 … bd36395 · spec docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-09-rgb-only-scale-free-trajectory-design.md

Bottom line. With depth removed from the input and from every loss, and the trajectory head predicting offsets in units of the anchor depth, the model now holds every SF3D record: MA 32.80 (signed 32.43) from the DCT chain (2D pretrain on HOI4D + EPIC + ARCTIC, then SF3D post-training, DCT head at both stages), and mask IoU 0.2738 plus direction accuracy 96.4 from the joint 2D+3D model, which is also the only model that keeps the hand sources. The previous record (31.13) needed depth. The 2D datasets lost nothing from the change; from scratch on SF3D, depth is still worth about 1 MA of geometry, which the 2D inits more than recover.

1The design change, in one paragraph

Depth had two jobs: an input feature (a small encoder fused into the FPN) and the one number that turned a 2D hand track into a metric target (the loss sampled the depth map at the track's first pixel and silently dropped rows where it read zero: 80% of ARCTIC, 100% of EPIC). Both are gone. The encoder is off (use_depth: false, load_depth: false); the projection loss anchors the GT first pixel at depth 1 (trajectory_proj_anchor: "unit"), exactly invariant to the unknown depth because the head predicts Δ̃ = Δ/z₀; on SF3D one multiply restores metres (GT z₀ during training, the model's own z_p at test). Verified by 11 unit tests, and the two test passes (predicted vs oracle scale) are identical on every reported metric because no test metric reads the trajectory scale. Explainers: docs/slides/2026-09-09_*explainer.html.

Reading

  • Depth from scratch: RGB scratch vs depth scratch (same DCT recipe): −1.1 MA, −4.3 PDet, −0.026 mIoU, +4° axis, +5 cm origin. The honest price on the one dataset with good depth.
  • 2D pretraining recovers it and more: every RGB init beats the depth record's neighbours on the headline metrics; the DCT chain is +1.7 MA over the depth record.
  • Do not re-initialise the readout: plain → DCT hand-over (30.07) lost the 2D-learned readout's last layer; keeping the head consistent (plain → plain 31.01, DCT → DCT 32.80) is worth 1 to 2.7 MA, and DCT at both stages also halves the flip rate and fixes the origin.
  • Joint vs chain: joint owns masks, detection and direction and keeps the hand sources; the chain owns articulation geometry (MA, origin, point depth, flips). They share the architecture.

Reading

  • Removing depth cost nothing on HOI4D (every metric improved vs the depth arm), and mixing EPIC and ARCTIC in with augmentation improved HOI4D again.
  • The DCT head removes the jitter (roughness 0.07 → 0.007) at equal HOI4D masks and the best 2D shape yet; the small sources lose some detection.
  • The type gate self-organises on EPIC (p_rev ≈ 0.9 on doors, ≈ 0.4 on drawers), which never happened on HOI4D alone.
  • Joint training keeps HOI4D at 0.676 / 85.2 where the chain forgets it, but its ×10 augmentation overfits EPIC and ARCTIC (their validation losses rise from epoch 3): the balance, not the learning rate, is the knob to tune.

Joint run, per-source validation loss: SF3D and HOI4D keep descending; EPIC and ARCTIC turn upward early.

5What was built

  • Scale-free head + unit anchor + no-depth loader (model/losses/geometric.py, train_OPDReal_better.py, train_SF3D_better.py, datasets/scenefun3d.py), 11 tests; an opt-in loader rule that slices depth columns off old checkpoints.
  • Multi-source datamodule (datasets/multisource_datamodule.py): any SF3D-format LMDBs, per-source scene split, per-source filters / repeat / augmentation / loss profile, source-homogeneous seeded batches, source tags on batches; entry train_multi_better.py.
  • Geometry-consistent augmentation (datasets/augment.py): photometric; horizontal flip with mirrored 2D fields, intrinsics, camera-frame 3D and the axis under the right-hand rule, left/right swapped in text (off for HOI4D, whose texts say "right drawer"); scale+translate crop that keeps the whole mask and point in frame (a change of intrinsics). 11 tests + a real-record check.
  • Joint 2D+3D training: per-batch loss profiles in the trainer (loss_profiles / source_profiles), per-source validation logging, checkpoint monitor on the SF3D validation loss.
  • Tooling: pod launch driver with lemon (clock) probe and auto-deleting watchers; panel tools honour the scale-free head; a cross-domain articulation probe tool.
  • Knowledge: three literature sweeps on smooth 2D-supervised trajectory heads and a synthesis (knowledge/2026-09-10_*); decision: DCT head from now on.

6Caveats

Every number is one seed; differences under about 0.5 MA, 1.5 PDet or 0.01 mIoU are within noise. The DCT-chain vs joint comparison also differs in total SF3D epochs seen (30 post-training vs 20 joint). EPIC's held-out split is 53 records. The test suite has no metric-trajectory-error number, so the scale-free head's metric size is only visible in the trajectory validation loss.
