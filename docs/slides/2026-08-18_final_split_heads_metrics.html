Gen-17 (08-18): split axis heads — the week's final structure, and where it stands

The last structural change of the week, and the smallest: one 3-vector axis readout becomes two. It produced the new overall-best checkpoint — best-ever type, origin, radius, and point metrics on top of gen-16's restored trajectories — and it is the base gen-17-2D's 2D-only experiment now trains on.

1 · The structure: why one axis head was wrong, and what replaced it

Before (gen-6 … gen-16): one readout, two physical quantities

Two different physical quantities forced through one output. (The rival explanation — the sigmoid-octant constraint — was tested first and refuted: the segmenter rescales to (−1, 1), full sphere reachable. The blend hypothesis survived; g16's measured 12.7% rot flip rate set the baseline.)

Gen-17: per-type readouts, shared trunk (split_axis_heads)

Full stack around it (finalized 08-16/17): frozen DINOv3 ViT-L/16 + dino.txt @512, v3 data, 59k split, origin local sample, normalized L_pp @0.1, GT-energy-normalized trajectory loss @0.5. RTX PRO 6000, 30 epochs, ~4 h, best = epoch 18 (val 0.9272 vs g16's 1.0123 — comparable composition, a real drop).

4 · Honest accounting

The goal it did NOT meet

The rot sign-flip rate — the metric that motivated the change — did not improve: 13.30% vs 12.73%. Flips are evidently not caused by cross-type interference; the model still cannot tell which way a fridge or window opens from appearance alone. Recorded candidates: sign-consistency with the predicted sweep, or handle-position cues.

The only cost

PDet −0.9 (21.44 → 20.56) with mIoU flat — reads as threshold noise around the 0.5-IoU gate, not a mask regression. Everything else improved or held.

GEN-17 IS THE NEW OVERALL BEST (best-epoch18-valloss0.9272.ckpt): gen-16's full-scale trajectories + record type / MA / origin / radius / point metrics. It closes the week's loop — the type-vs-geometry entanglement that the twist era paid for with |ω| hedging is now resolved structurally, one readout per physical quantity — and it is the base gen-17-2D's 2D-only supervision experiment trains on.
