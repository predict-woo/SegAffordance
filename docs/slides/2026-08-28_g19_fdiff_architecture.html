g19_fdiff — model structure

Best-articulation checkpoint (20260821_sf3d_g19_fdiff, MA 29.9, traj_dir 96.1). Frozen DINOv3 trunk + text-gated FPN + transformer decoder, g17 split articulation heads, plain 20-point trajectory head trained under the gen-19 first-difference losses. Class: model/segmenter.py:CRIS.

What makes it g19_fdiff: the architecture is exactly the g17 recipe (DINOv3@512 + split axis heads + heatmap-lift 3D) with an unchanged plain trajectory head — the generation's change is purely the loss: the three first-difference terms on the trajectory segments (velocity / segment-angle / segment-length). Trainable parameters are everything outside the two frozen towers: depth encoder, FPN, decoder, projector, word_proj, and the five MLP heads. Still the best-articulation checkpoint (MA 29.9); the closed-form variant below showed the trajectory machinery can be replaced by two Gram quadratics at MA 29.19.

Closed-form variant — what changes

20260828_sf3d_closedform (MA 29.19, origin record 0.250). Everything from the inputs down to the shared condition vector is identical — the diagram below starts there. The trajectory head and every loss that touched a trajectory are removed; in their place, one closed-form loss on the articulation predictions themselves (model/losses/geometric.py:closed_form_screw_loss).

The closed-form loss is the N→∞ limit of "decode the screw curve from the predicted articulation, compare to the GT curve" — the integrals collapse so no curve is ever built. Two residual vectors per revolute row, computed from the predicted axis n̂, origin q, and 3D point p₀ against their GT counterparts: radial Δr = r − r* with r = (I − n̂n̂ᵀ)(p₀ − q), and tangential Δt = t − t* with t = n̂ × r (the sign carrier). Then, each normalized by the GT curve's own magnitude:

position (L²):  [(3π/4 − 2)‖Δr‖² + (π/4)‖Δt‖² − Δr·Δt] / [(π − 2)‖r*‖²]
derivative (H¹): [(π/4)(‖Δr‖² + ‖Δt‖²) − Δr·Δt] / [(π/2)‖r*‖²]
prismatic rows (both terms): ‖d̂ − d̂*‖² = 2(1 − cos∠)

Net effect: zero added parameters, zero trajectory machinery — yet MA 20.4 → 29.19 over the same architecture without the loss (arm B), 0.7 shy of g19_fdiff's 29.9, and a new origin record (0.250 vs 0.257). The entire benefit of trajectory co-training, distilled into two named quadratics on the articulation heads. Trade-off: concedes matched-axis sharpness (22.3° vs 17.9° for the sampled fdiff decode).
