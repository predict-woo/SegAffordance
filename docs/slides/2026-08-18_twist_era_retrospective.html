The Twist Era (gen-2 → gen-5): why we ended it

The se(3) twist (ω, v) was the sole predicted motion representation: type read from |ω| (rotation ⇔ |ω| ≈ 1, translation ⇔ |ω| ≈ 0), radius decoded hyperbolically from it. That one design choice — a regression magnitude carrying a discrete label — created a hedging failure that three generations of machinery reduced but never removed. The numbers below are the case for retiring it.

1 · The structural problem: hedging |ω| is geometry, not calibration

Twist: type inside a regression target

Under type ambiguity, the MSE-optimal |ω| is a probability-weighted average — a value that means neither type, and that the hyperbolic radius decode amplifies into a ~10× radius error. Hedging has a direct geometric cost.

CE head: hedging is free and harmless

Cross-entropy hedging is calibrated uncertainty with zero geometric side effect. This is the gen-6 design in one sentence: put each quantity in the output space that suits it.

3 · Gen-4 (08-11) — body metric + K=4 WTA: trajectory fixed, ω hedge survived the machinery

What the machinery fixed
  • Trajectory un-parked: winner MSE 0.0099 vs baseline 0.018
  • traj_dir_acc 79.7 → 82.7%, cos 0.508 → 0.546
  • WTA healthy: hypothesis spread 0.452, all 4 bundles alive (win shares 0.21–0.28), selector 56% vs 25% random
  • mIoU 0.103 → 0.118 (best CLIP arm to date)
What it did not fix
  • Even the oracle bundle's revolute |ω| ≈ 0.08 (selected: 0.10)
  • Radius ratio median 9.6× — 98% of samples inflated
  • Only 6% of revolute samples decoded as rotation
  • Type-from-|ω| 52.0% (down from 60.2), axis err 48.4° (up from 42.8)
Measured cause, not conjecture: at ρ = 0.25 a full ω-hedge cost only ~0.03 of WTA distortion (0.5·ρ²·1) — the cells split along the expensive dimensions (field term, 4.0-weighted trajectory) and averaging ω inside each cell stayed nearly free. Bonus failure: the literature anneal schedule (T0=10) collapsed all 4 hypotheses onto one point (pairwise distance 0.005) — a symmetric fixed point WTA cannot escape; aborted at epoch 9 and relaunched with T0=0.5.

4 · Gen-5 (08-12) — maximum effort: it worked, and that is the problem

Price paid to make ω supervisable
  • twist_metric_rho 0.25 → 0.75: hedge cost ~0.03 → ~0.28 of distortion
  • min_revolute_radius: 0.10 — the entire knob/dial/faucet class deleted: 40% of revolute records, 417,765 → 356,747. Knobs supervised ω through pure ambiguity (pred |ω| 0.06 knob vs 0.25 door, corr 0.58)
  • Eval split filtered too — metrics no longer comparable to gen-3/4
Best twist results ever
  • Revolute |ω| median 0.59 vs prismatic 0.10 (clean 6× separation)
  • Decoded-as-rot 6% → 61%; radius ratio 9.6 → 2.13
  • Type-from-|ω| 89.4%, axis err 29.4°, traj_dir 92.6% / cos 0.750
Residuals at the ceiling
  • |ω| still 41% short of 1, unsaturated after 16 epochs (0.30 → 0.53 → 0.59, still climbing)
  • Radius still 2.1× off
  • mIoU regressed to 0.090 (g4: 0.118)
  • Type still 5.6 pts below gen-2's plain CE head

6 · The decision (gen-6 spec, 08-13)

Gen-5 proved the WTA + body-metric machinery can fight the type-in-|ω| hedge to |ω| = 0.59 — "but that is a permanent tax paid to make a regression target carry a discrete label." Two design specs, 4 hypothesis bundles, an annealing schedule, a tuned metric, and 40% of the revolute data thrown away bought 89.4% type accuracy; a plain CE head — which structurally cannot hedge geometrically — had already delivered 95% in gen-2, and delivered 95.3% (best ever) in gen-17.
Gen-6 replacement: each quantity in its natural output space — type as CE logits, axis as a normalized 3-vector, origin as an explicit 3D point (radius computed linearly from it), trajectory as K=1 direct readout. The twist code paths remain in the repo, config-gated, so gen-3/4/5 stay reproducible. Kept from the era: the fast anneal lesson, the radius filter, and the trajectory-hedging diagnosis that later motivated gen-16's normalized trajectory loss.
