The two model changes this week

g17: split axis heads  ·  g19: truncated-DCT trajectory head — everything else this week was losses, data, or training recipes.

1 · g17 — split axis heads

BEFORE (g16)
One vector must serve two physically orthogonal quantities: a door's hinge axis is vertical while a drawer's slide is horizontal. When the type is ambiguous (drawer vs door), gradients from both interpretations blend into the same weights — the head learns an average of two motions.
AFTER (g17)
Two independent readouts off the shared hidden layer. Each head only ever receives gradients for its own motion type, so hinge and slide geometry stop interfering. Legacy consumers still see one motion_pred — the row-wise selection fills it.
type 95.3 (+3.0)
MA 25.9 (+2.8)
matched axis 16.9° (best at the time)
origin/radius records
rot flip rate unchanged — that tail needed its own idea

2 · g19 — truncated-DCT trajectory head

BEFORE (raw points)
Roughness (mean second difference) 0.0915 m — ~28× the GT floor of 0.0032. Losses can only dampen jitter after the fact (fdiff got 1.8×; the head got 10×).
AFTER (g19 DCT)
The head emits 6 DCT coefficients per axis; a fixed orthonormal inverse-DCT decodes them into 20 points. Point-to-point jitter is outside the representable set — smoothness is architectural, not a loss term to balance.
roughness 0.0915 → 0.0090 (10×; GT floor 0.0032)
mIoU 0.2685 + PDet 21.72 (records at the time)
helped 2D even more: shape 0.0947, best 2D arm
small trade: matched axis +1.3°
Not pictured: the g21 screw-direction term and the fdiff losses — both loss changes, not architecture (dir term failed; fdiff holds the MA record 29.9). The analytic-decode arm currently training removes the trajectory head entirely — if it matches arm D, change 2's head may become a decode.
