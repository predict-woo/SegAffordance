DINOv3 stack finalization (08-16 → 08-17): baseline, three staged bets, one fix

Method first: a two-agent literature sweep (knowledge/dinov3-dense-adapter-survey.md) produced ONE spec with three independent, flag-gated stages — Resolution 512, DPT-style Taps, text Cost map — run as sequential single-flag experiments for clean pairwise attribution on the gen-12 baseline. Two days later the finalized structure is g12 + R + normalized trajectory loss: one stage adopted, one rejected, one deferred, and the recipe's one latent defect (rot-sweep collapse) fixed. All arms: frozen DINOv3 ViT-L/16 + dino.txt, v3 data, 59k split, 5,088 test samples.

1 · Gen-12 (08-16) — the baseline: dino.txt wins "where", loses "which way"

Every localization metric: family best (vs g11b CLIP)
  • 2D point 0.1138 (−28%), 3D point 0.2529 m (−17%)
  • origin vs q* / line 0.3259 / 0.2964 m (−3.6 / −2.2 cm)
  • mIoU 0.1599 (+1.3 pts), radius 0.1734 (tied)
The axis head is the casualty
  • axis° all / matched 37.1 / 29.1 (CLIP: 28.1 / 14.6) — matched error doubled, MA pass halved (12.1 vs 22.6)
  • normalized L_pp probe 0.199 (CLIP family ~0.054) — the triad is far less mutually consistent, dominated by axis error
  • Suspect: the single-final-layer pyramid starves the direction readout — exactly what stage T was designed to test

4 · Gen-16 (08-17): the collapse fix that completed the structure

The defect

Every w=0.15 arm predicted near-zero rotational sweeps: g13's mean rot sweep 0.067 m on val93 and 0.041 m on val1684 — flat arcs masquerading as trajectories, while flat-looking traj losses hid it. Cause: the raw MSE trajectory loss prices a full-scale wrong-direction arc higher than a collapsed one; at w=0.15 collapse is the cheap minimum.

Pre-launch validation (cheap, decisive)

A 300-step fine-tune probe on g13's collapsed checkpoint (tools/diag_traj_collapse_test.py): with the normalized loss, rot sweeps grew 0.083 → 0.192 m (22/26 samples >1.5×); the old-recipe control shrank them further 0.083 → 0.022 m (0/26). Hypothesis confirmed before spending a full run.

The fix & result — all spec gates passed
  • Per-row GT-energy-normalized trajectory loss, weight restored to 0.5
  • Rot sweeps restored: val93 0.436 m, val1684 0.791 m (gate: >0.2)
  • traj_dir 94.46% / cos 0.798 — project-best direction
  • g13 records held (mIoU 0.2636, 2D point 0.0988 best-ever); price: MA −3.1, matched axis +1.1°

5 · The finalized structure

Adopted stack (the gen-17/18 base): frozen DINOv3 ViT-L/16 + dino.txt · input 512 (stage R) · single-final-layer pyramid (no taps — stage T rejected: at 512 the final layer keeps enough spatial detail, early taps dilute the semantically-aligned features) · no cost map (stage C deferred — its origin/radius/traj_dir gains are real but confounded with the taps; "g13 + cost map without taps" is a recorded candidate) · v3 data, 59k split · origin local sample · normalized L_pp @0.1 · GT-energy-normalized trajectory loss @0.5 (gen-16). Gen-16 best-epoch21 = the new overall-best checkpoint: g13-class masks + real full-scale trajectories.
Also banked from g16's checkpoint (08-18): the sign-aware axis re-test — signed axis error 33.9° vs 28.3° unsigned, flip rate 12.7% on rotational samples (door swings predicted in the wrong hemisphere) — the measured baseline that motivated gen-17's split axis heads.
